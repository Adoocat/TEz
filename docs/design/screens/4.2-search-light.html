Özet (Abstract) Çevir (TR)
"Automated code review using large language models promises to alleviate the burden on software maintainers. In this paper, we conduct an extensive empirical study across 12 open-source repositories inspecting 4,200 pull requests. Our findings indicate that while GPT-4 achieves 74% precision on syntactic issue detection, human reviewers still identify semantic race conditions at a significantly higher rate..."
