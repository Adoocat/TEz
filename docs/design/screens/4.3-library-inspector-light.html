Evaluating LLM Generated Code Reviews in Industrial Settings: An Empirical Study

person Ahmet Şahin, Ece Güneş, Michael R. Smith
IEEE/ACM ICSE 2024 · Yıl: 2024 · Peer-Reviewed
DOI: 10.1145/3611643.3616281
verified Dahil (Aşama 2: Tam Metin · 14 Eki 2026)
Özet Metni en · 248 kelime

Automated code review powered by modern Large Language Models (LLMs) offers significant potential to reduce developer workload during continuous integration workflows. In this paper, we conduct an extensive empirical study across three tier-1 software companies evaluating 4,200 real-world pull requests. Our findings indicate that while LLMs identify stylistic defects with 82% precision, semantic logical regressions remain undetected unless augmented with dynamic symbolic trace context.
